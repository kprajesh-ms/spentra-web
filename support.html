Common questions

How do I back up my data?

Settings → Backup & Restore → Create backup. Spentra creates a single zip with your databases and receipt images. The iOS share sheet appears so you can save it to Files (which includes iCloud Drive), Google Drive, email, AirDrop, or anywhere else. We recommend keeping at least one off-device copy.

I bought the Lifetime Unlock — how do I restore it on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID, install Spentra, then open Settings → Lifetime Unlock → Restore Purchases.

The exchange rate looks wrong.

Spentra uses public daily rates from open.er-api.com and caches them for 24 hours. You can override the rate for any individual transaction by toggling "Override exchange rate" on the Add Expense screen.

OCR didn't pick up the right total.

Receipt formats vary widely. Spentra's OCR creates a draft transaction with its best guess; you confirm or correct everything before saving. Settings → Drafts shows pending receipts.

Can I keep separate Personal and Business records?

Yes — with the Lifetime Unlock, Settings → Databases lets you create multiple isolated databases. Switch between them anytime; data does not mix.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall Spentra. iOS removes the entire app sandbox, including the database and any saved receipt images. Backups you exported to your own cloud are unaffected; manage those yourself.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of network calls Spentra makes (exchange rates, Apple's IAP, RevenueCat receipt validation — none of which include your transactions).
