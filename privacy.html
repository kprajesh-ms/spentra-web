Privacy Policy

Spentra · Effective 6 May 2026 · Last updated 6 May 2026

Spentra is a local-first expense tracker. We are designed so that the developer never sees your financial data. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device, what (very little) leaves it, and the permissions Spentra asks for and why.

1. The short version

  • Every transaction, category, budget, and receipt image you create is stored only on your device.
  • We do not run servers that hold your data. We do not have user accounts.
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking data.
  • The only network calls Spentra makes are: (a) public exchange-rate lookups, (b) Apple's in-app purchase validation, and (c) RevenueCat's purchase-status check (no personal data sent).

2. Data we store on your device

The following are saved in app storage on your iPhone:

  • Transactions you log: amount, currency, category, date, optional note, optional merchant, optional contact name for splits.
  • Receipt images you scan, compressed and saved as JPEG files in Spentra's app folder.
  • Budgets and category structures you create.
  • App preferences: language, default currency, dashboard view (month/year).
  • Cached exchange rates (so the app works offline).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or back it up using the app's Backup or Export features, in which case the destination (Files, iCloud Drive, email, AirDrop, etc.) is chosen by you.

3. Permissions Spentra requests

  • Camera — only when you tap the scan icon to capture a receipt. Images are saved locally and processed on-device. No image is uploaded.
  • Contacts — only when you tap the contact icon while adding a split, to attach a name to a split. Spentra reads only the contact you select.
  • Photo Library — used only if you choose to attach a saved receipt image instead of scanning a new one.

You can decline or revoke any of these in iOS Settings; the app remains functional with manual entry.

4. What we send over the network

  • Exchange rates. When you record a transaction in a non-base currency, Spentra fetches the latest published rate from open.er-api.com. We send only the currency code (e.g. USD); we do not send the amount, your identity, or any transaction detail.
  • In-app purchase. If you buy the Lifetime Unlock, the purchase is processed by Apple. Apple's privacy practices apply to that transaction and are documented at apple.com/legal/privacy. Spentra does not see your Apple ID or payment details.
  • RevenueCat. Spentra uses RevenueCat to verify whether your Apple ID has the Lifetime Unlock entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-installation ID and the receipt issued by Apple. No transactions, names, or financial data are sent.

That is the complete list. Spentra makes no other network calls.

5. On-device receipt scanning (OCR)

Receipt scanning uses Apple's on-device text-recognition APIs. The image and the recognised text remain on your phone. No part of a scanned receipt is uploaded to us, to Apple, or to anyone else.

6. Children's privacy

Spentra is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK / EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children because we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

7. International users (UAE, UK, EU, India)

Because your data is stored only on your device, no cross-border transfer of your personal data occurs. You are the controller of your own data. If you wish to delete it, uninstall Spentra; this removes the local database and all receipt images. Backups you have exported to your own cloud storage are managed by you.

8. Security

Spentra relies on iOS sandboxing and full-device encryption (enabled on every modern iPhone) to protect your data at rest. Backup zip files you create are not encrypted by Spentra; protect them as you would any other personal file.

9. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, where required, prompt you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.
